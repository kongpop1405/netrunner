ep5 vs ep6v2

Same skeleton (Fast Start only, no jump/slide, pre_play + guard chain). ep5 buys nothing and quits at box 2; ep6v2 buys all 3 boosts and is meant to quit at box 3 but still reuses the x2 marker — x3 template capture deferred.

Guard chain = 6 run-bots

boxrun_speed, boxrun_passive, boxrun_magnet, boxrun_magnet_v2, coinrun, and xpstat all carry the ported Send-Life + Level-Up mid-run popup guard. sendlife/addfriend/giftdraw are single-purpose menu loops and don't need it.

Boost spend risk

boxrun_magnet, boxrun_speed, coinrun, and boxrun_magnet_v2 all spend coins on a boost every run (multi-buy roll or fixed tiles). ep5 and xpstat are the only two run-bots that buy nothing.
